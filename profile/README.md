*DJI Pilot 2 is a drone flight control app for Windows that combines a live camera view, flight telemetry, and mission-planning tools for pilots of compatible DJI aircraft.*

DJI Pilot 2 for Windows puts the camera feed, flight data, and aircraft settings on a single screen, aimed at pilots who'd rather not switch between separate tools mid-flight. The DJI Pilot 2 desktop experience appeals to enterprise and hobbyist operators alike, letting them plan routes ahead of time and review footage once a flight wraps up.

## Why people use it
| Feature | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| Live camera feed | See exactly what the aircraft's camera sees while flying |
| Flight telemetry | Track altitude, speed, battery, and signal strength in real time |
| Mission planning | Lay out waypoints and routes before takeoff |
| Camera and gimbal controls | Adjust camera settings and gimbal position mid-flight |
| Flight log review | Look back at past flights and recorded footage after landing |

## What it needs
→ **OS:** Windows 10, 64-bit, or later
→ **Processor:** A modern 64-bit processor
→ **Memory:** Enough RAM to run video and telemetry processing smoothly
→ **Storage:** Free disk space for the installer and for saved flight logs

## Setting up
1. Download the installer using the button above.
2. Run it and follow the on-screen setup steps.
3. Connect a compatible DJI remote controller or aircraft before your first launch.

## Getting going
The first time you open DJI Pilot 2, work through the built-in pre-flight checklist, confirm your controller and aircraft are connected, and review the telemetry panel before taking off. From there, most pilots set up a waypoint route for their first mission flight.

## Questions people ask
- [ ] **Is DJI Pilot 2 free to use?** — Yes, DJI Pilot 2 is free to use; a compatible DJI aircraft is required for the flight-control features to do anything.
- [ ] **Does it work with my DJI aircraft?** — It depends on the model — DJI Pilot 2 supports a range of DJI enterprise and consumer aircraft, so check DJI's compatibility list before you count on it working with yours.
- [ ] **Can I plan a flight route ahead of time?** — Yes, the mission-planning tools let you lay out waypoints before you take off.

## Licensing
DJI Pilot 2 is proprietary software from DJI, offered as a free download rather than under an open-source license.
